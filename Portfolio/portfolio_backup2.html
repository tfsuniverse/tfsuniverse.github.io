The Journey

The Foundation as an Engineer (2014 - 2017) +

The journey began in Ahmedabad during his primary schooling at Som-Lalit School, where Mr. Oza started getting curious about High performance Supercars & Superbikes and so he started pursuing his interest in the Automotive world on the Internet. Later he finished his SSC (10th Standard) in 2014. Due to his passion in Machines & Vehicles he chose a specialized technical path instead of a traditional high school route. He then moved to Baroda to study for a Diploma in Automobile Engineering at Parul University.

He was an Hyperactive student who liked to participate in multiple activities & opportunities. From the very beginning he had a daily habit to research on various topics of Automotive engineering. Additionally he also took multiple internships from multiple Automotive giants such as Mercedes - benz, MV Agusta, Benelli, Hyosung & Honda. This gave him a deep understanding of Internal Combustion (IC) Engines & Modern Automotive Technologies. However while pursuing Internship at MV Agusta, Benelli & Hyosung he found that his inclination was now moving slightly more towards the Superbikes.

Advanced Research & Navigating his Innovations (2017 - 2026) +

In 2017 Mr. Oza returned to Ahmedabad for a B.Tech in Automobile Engineering at Indus University, where he graduated in 2020. During these years, he shifted focus from learning to creating the Standard & Innovative High performance IC Engines & its Technologies.

During his Graduation Period he also got expertise in space planning and designing in the field of Interior designing by designing his Studio (a room of his personal interest) by himself. He came up with a theme of the Interior considering his interest in automotive vehicles, music & mountains. He used Multiple custom paints, artworks, Actual sculptures, Motorcycles & the parts of motorcycles in his designs which eventually became a homely vibe for him.

In 2017, Mr. Oza began his journey as a Designing Engineer after which he designed & developed his first ever Motorcycle called the SE1. SE1 was a Hybrid design of Bobber & Brat Style Motorcycles which itself was an innovation... Mr. Oza later named this distinctive design philosophy "BobArt" and secured its intellectual property.

Mr. Oza conceptualized and engineered the V.ODNE1s High-Performance Engine Platform, designing multiple conventional and innovative V-Series engine architectures... Under Mr. Oza's leadership, Oza Motorsports developed the proprietary LTB Valve Technology—a Camless Valve Mechanism.

Later he realized he needed to protect his creations, Mr. Oza pursued a 3 - month course on IPR... which strategically protects his creative ideas & his technologies.

The Struggles, the Pivots, & Government Backing (2020 - 2024) +

Finding funding for an automotive hardware startup is notoriously tough, but Mr. Oza began pitching steadily to incubation committees. His first breakthrough came when ICSII was newly established.

Soon after he received Mentorship from GUSEC. Then in 2023 SUSEC also decided to fund his project... In 2024 after almost 1.5 Years of Pitching to i-Hub Gujarat he secured his biggest funding and opportunities as he received a Non returnable Government Grant of 7,75,000/- INR and an Offer from Belgium Government to Re-locate his company to Brussels.

Higher Achievements & Global Recognition (2025 - Present) +

By managing a diverse team and establishing a strong corporate structure, Mr. Oza successfully set up Oza Motorsports Private Limited as a well-organized corporate entity in India. In 2025, i-Hub invited him again to share his Success Story.

Today, Mr. Oza stands at a significant global crossroads... leading to a formal offer from the Government of Belgium to relocate his entire company from Ahmedabad, India, to Brussels, Belgium signifying his shift from a local engineering student to an internationally recognized Automotive inventor.

The Journey

The 16-Year-Old Superbike Broker Who Couldn't Ride +

Long before earning his engineering degree, Mr. Oza started his career in premium automotive sales at just the age of 16. He entered into the premium Pre-owned Superbike market as an independent broker, facing a unique challenge of being too young & didn’t know how to ride a motorcycle.

Despite this, he successfully sold his first superbike, which was a Benelli 600i. Over the following years, he continued facilitating the sale of numerous motorcycles, strengthening his industry network while developing extensive technical and market knowledge.

Scaling from a Broker to Dealer +

As his network expanded, Mr. Oza grew from a young broker to a well-known premium pre-owned superbike dealer. Over the years, he developed a wide portfolio, mastering the specific sales, valuation, and insurance consultation skills needed for global brands.

He didn't just sell bikes; he learned about the technicalities, the features, the advancement, the machinery & even how premium dealerships operate. He later added insurance consulting to his skill set.

Sales Executive & Marshall at BMW Motorrad +

On 22nd February 2021, Mr. Oza joined BMW Motorrad, marking his transition from an independent premium superbike dealer to the corporate luxury automotive industry. Over the course of 15 months, he was entrusted with one of BMW Motorrad's key territories Gujarat.

He generated 69 bookings and completed 64 successful retail deliveries. His consistent focus resulted in an outstanding average customer satisfaction rating of 9.8/10. He was subsequently appointed as a Regional Marshal for BMW Motorrad, representing the brand across the Gujarat territory.
