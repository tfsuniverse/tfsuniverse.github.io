●​ The Foundation as an Engineer (2014 - 2017): 
 
The journey began in Ahmedabad during his primary schooling at Som-Lalit 
School, where Mr. Oza started getting curious about High performance 
Supercars & Superbikes and so he started pursuing his interest in the 
Automotive world on the Internet. Later he finished his SSC (10th Standard) in 
2014. Due to his passion in Machines & Vehicles he chose a specialized 
technical path instead of a traditional high school route. He then moved to 
Baroda to study for a Diploma in Automobile Engineering at Parul University. 
 
He was an Hyperactive student who liked to participate in multiple activities & 
opportunities. From the very beginning he had a daily habit to research on 
various topics of Automotive engineering. Additionally he also took multiple 
internships from multiple Automotive giants such as Mercedes - benz, MV 
Agusta, Benelli, Hyosung & Honda. This gave him a deep understanding of 
Internal Combustion (IC) Engines & Modern Automotive Technologies. 
However while pursuing Internship at MV Agusta, Benelli & Hyosung he found 
that his inclination was now moving slightly more towards the Superbikes. 
 
 
●​ Advanced Research & Navigating his Innovations and his Startup 
(2017 - 2026):  
 
In 2017 Mr. Oza returned to Ahmedabad for a B.Tech in Automobile 
Engineering at Indus University, where he graduated in 2020. During these 
years, he shifted focus from learning to creating the Standard & Innovative 
High performance IC Engines & its Technologies.  
 
During his Graduation Period he also got expertise in space planning and 
designing in the field of Interior designing by designing his Studio (a room of 
his personal interest) by himself. He came up with a theme of the Interior 
considering his interest in automotive vehicles, music & mountains. He used 
Multiple custom paints, artworks, Actual sculptures, Motorcycles & the parts of 
motorcycles in his designs which eventually became a homely vibe for him. This 
later in 2025 helped him to design another Office & Studio for both of his 
companies.  
 
In 2017, Mr. Oza began his journey as a Designing Engineer after which he 
designed & developed his first ever Motorcycle called the SE1. SE1 was a 
Hybrid design of Bobber & Brat Style Motorcycles which itself was an 
innovation. Apart from this he also gave a Rat bite look to the Motorcycle. Mr. 
Oza later named this distinctive design philosophy "BobArt" and secured its 
intellectual property by registering the design under the Designs Act, 2000 
 
Mr. Oza conceptualized and engineered the V.ODNE1s High-Performance 
Engine Platform, designing multiple conventional and innovative V-Series 
engine architectures. Through this platform, he introduced a proprietary design 
methodology that unlocked V-engine configurations in multiples of three (3, 
6, 9, 12, 15, 18...) which significantly expanded the design possibilities beyond 
conventional V-engine architecture.  
 
Under Mr. Oza's leadership, guidance, and technical supervision, Oza 
Motorsports developed the proprietary LTB Valve Technology—a Camless 
Valve Mechanism—positioning the company among only two automotive 
organizations globally to own this advanced valve actuation technology.  
 
By 2018, Mr. Oza also started working as a Scientist as he actively started 
working on his own innovations in IC engines & its technologies. Later for

& Innovations). 
 
Soon he started to form a team for which he himself conducted multiple
