At The Flying Shirt, we don't just read spec sheets. We ride. We believe in providing the most honest, comprehensive motorcycle reviews and superbike content in India.

Our review methodology evaluates every machine in real-world scenarios, answering the questions that actually matter to riders.

Performance

Engine dynamics, braking, handling, and high-speed stability.

Comfort & Ergonomics

Rider triangle, seat cushioning, and touring capability.

Technology

Electronics, IMU systems, quickshifters, and dash interface.

Ownership

Maintenance costs, reliability, and real-world mileage.
