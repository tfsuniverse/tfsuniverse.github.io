Frequently Asked Questions

What is Soochh?

Soochh is an independent music and creative storytelling brand within the TFS Universe. We focus on original songs that value emotions, thoughtful lyrics, and human connection.

Where can I listen to Soochh's songs?

Our music is available globally on major streaming platforms including Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Amazon Music, and JioSaavn.

Is Soochh part of TFS Universe?

Yes, Soochh operates as the dedicated independent music and audio storytelling label within the broader TFS Universe creative ecosystem.

Can independent artists collaborate with Soochh?

Yes, we are always open to creative collaborations with singers, songwriters, producers, and filmmakers who share our vision for meaningful storytelling. Please reach out via our contact page.
