Creative Breakdown

Soundscape Exploration

Deconstructing the DNA of the SOOCHH sonic identity — where raw emotion meets cinematic production.

waves

Textural Layering

We utilize multi-layered granular synthesis to create "organic digital" textures. By sampling acoustic instruments and stretching them across time, we achieve a sound that feels both ancient and futuristic — a tactile grit that defines the SOOCHH atmosphere.

heart_broken

Melodic Vulnerability

Melodies are never static. We employ microtonal shifts and purposeful imperfections in timing to mimic human frailty. These "vulnerabilities" in the digital signal create a bridge of empathy between the machine-driven production and the listener's ear.

The Founder

Dhairya N. Oza

As the visionary behind SOOCHH, Dhairya N. Oza operates at the intersection of narrative depth and digital sonic landscapes. His work seeks to bridge the gap between abstract philosophical concepts and tangible auditory experiences.

"I don't just want people to hear music; I want them to feel the architecture of a thought. Sound is the only medium fast enough to keep up with the speed of emotion."

With a background in both classical theory and avant-garde electronic production, Dhairya focuses on creating "living" compositions that evolve with every listen.

Artist's Musical Journey

Beyond The Sound

The SOOCHH project is more than just a collection of frequencies; it's a commitment to pushing the boundaries of sonic storytelling. We believe that music should be a transformative experience — one that challenges the listener to perceive their environment through a new lens of digital organicism.
